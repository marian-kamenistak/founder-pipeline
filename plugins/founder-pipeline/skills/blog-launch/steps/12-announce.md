# Step 12 — Announce the post (owned channels)

Syndication (step 10) puts copies elsewhere; this step tells the owner's OWN audience. Runs after deploy, ∥ steps 10-11. All copy on **your top-tier model** (reader-facing = never cheap); identity channels are drafts for the owner to paste, never auto-posted.

## 1. Professional social network (the main lever — 1 main + 2-5 smaller posts)

Draft in the owner's voice via `/founder-pipeline:brand-voice`. If you have a dedicated social-posting skill for the platform, use it; otherwise draft inline against these rules, which are what such a skill would enforce: short fragments, one thought per line, and no link in the first line if the platform penalises it for reach — put the link in the comments or after the hook instead.

- **1 main post**: the post's contrarian claim + the personal story hook + link. Tag the people who inspired it, reviewed it, or are named in it.
- **2-5 smaller posts**: each takes ONE idea/quote from the post and stands alone; the blog link rides in comments. Space them over the following 1-2 weeks; propose a posting schedule.
- Save all drafts to `exports/social/` (main.md, small-01.md, ...).

## 2. Communities (owner pastes manually)

Prepare a 2-3 sentence blurb per community, tone-flexed (warm, no marketing smell): the owner's own community spaces plus any external groups they belong to. Ask the owner which ones this post genuinely fits rather than blasting all of them. Save to `exports/communities.md`.

## 3. Newsletter

Queue the post for the next newsletter issue: one-paragraph teaser + link, saved to `exports/newsletter.md`. Don't send anything; the newsletter has its own cadence.

## 4. Optional repurposing backlog (note, don't execute)

Long tail worth logging in `STATE.md` for later: top quotes → carousel/video with subtitles, and a pitch to a relevant podcast host if the topic fits. Only flag it; separate sessions own these.

Report to the owner: social drafts + proposed schedule, community blurbs awaiting their pick, newsletter teaser queued. Mark the pipeline COMPLETE in `STATE.md`.
