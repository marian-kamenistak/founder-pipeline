# Money model — Fermi decomposition per bet (step 6)

Purpose: give viability decisions (`/founder-pipeline:founder` P2.S1 Economics, P2.S3 Pricing, P2.S4 Validation) a cited revenue RANGE per bet — input, never proof. A demand signal without a price attached cannot answer "will this sell at {currency}X", which is the question at ideation. Money figures here are written `{currency}` — substitute the currency declared once at P1.S1.

## The decomposition (all four factors, no skipping)

```
12-mo revenue = reachable audience × problem incidence × conversion to paid × price
```

| Factor | How to source it | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Reachable audience** | ICP size the owner can actually touch in 12 months (network + locked channels + realistic content reach) — NOT the TAM. Cite the count's origin (professional-network search result count, community size, list size) | `[source]` or `[assumption]` |
| **Problem incidence** | share of that audience with the problem NOW — from the corroboration silos (job postings mentioning the pain, discourse volume relative to audience) | usually `[assumption — validation: <outreach reply rate / interview>]` |
| **Conversion to paid** | base rate from comparable motions (cold outreach → paid: low single digits; warm network: ~an order higher). Cite the comparable, take the conservative end | `[source: comparable]` |
| **Price** | REAL benchmark: competitor pricing pages, marketplace rates, salary-equivalent math. Never invent; if no benchmark exists, that's a finding ("unpriced category") not a blank to fill | `[source]` |

## Output per bet

- **Range, not point**: compute at conservative and optimistic ends of the two weakest factors. If the range spans >10×, say the model is not yet decision-grade and name which factor to validate first.
- **Dominant-uncertainty line**: which single factor moves the result most — that factor is the next validation target (this is what the founder run's P2.S4 minimum-evidence backlog should pick up).
- **Floor comparison**: put the conservative end next to the pre-registered money floor (step 2). Below floor at conservative + mid at optimistic = the bet funds a content angle, not a stream — say so plainly.

## Refusals

- No factor without a tag. A bare number anywhere in the money block invalidates the block.
- No summing correlated bets into a "portfolio revenue" — correlated bets don't diversify (rubric §6).
- No survey-stated willingness-to-pay as the price source — pricing-page and paid-comparable evidence only (stated-WTP inflates; `/founder-pipeline:pricing` owns real WTP testing later).
