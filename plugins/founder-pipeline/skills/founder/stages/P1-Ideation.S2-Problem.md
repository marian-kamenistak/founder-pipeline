# P1.S2 — Problem & opportunity → `P1-Ideation.S2-Problem.md`

**Phase: P1-Ideation**

**Earns its keep when** the problem is novel, contested, or shifting (AI-era engineering hiring, post-FTX consumer trust). When the user has a felt sense but can't articulate the shift.

**Becomes performative when** the problem is obvious and the user already has 50+ customers paying for an inferior alternative. Skip to ICP.

**Start from the intake:** read the intake digest (folder path recorded in `brief.md`, digest `INTAKE.md` inside it) and the raw assets it points to first, and pre-draft answers from the user's own words — then ask only what the assets don't answer.

**Ask one at a time:**
1. Customer pain in one sentence — no jargon, no business-speak.
2. Why now? What's changing in the world that creates the opening? (cite at least one shift: regulatory / technological / behavioral / economic)
3. Why isn't this solved already? A RAW list of alternatives from the user's own knowledge — names + a failure hypothesis per alternative. Do NOT research them here; P1.S5 researches and validates this list.
4. "I've been thinking about this for years" evidence — origin story.
5. What would have to be true about the world for this stream to NOT work?
6. Money proximity — who pays TODAY to avoid or patch this pain? Name the behavior, tool spend, or budget line. No current spend and no budget owner means the pain may be real but unfunded.

**Skills:** `/brainstorm` if the user wants to think through the why-now angle. (Alternatives research belongs to P1.S5 — don't `/scout` them here.)

**Write to** `P1-Ideation.S2-Problem.md`. Every market-shift claim gets `[source: ...]` or `[assumption — validation: ...]`.

**Def-of-success — ALL must pass before owner sign-off (fail → improvement loop per SKILL.md, never advance):**
- `/critical` run recorded; the score is advisory (target ≥ 8/10) — the gate is the PASS/FAIL criteria below, not the number.
- Pain statement ≤ 25 words, zero jargon — the ICP would say it back verbatim.
- Pain statement traceable to a quoted line or named asset in the intake digest (`INTAKE.md`).
- ≥ 1 cited market shift with a dated, checkable source — `[source: <title>, <date>, <url>]`; the critical pass verifies the source exists before PASS.
- ≥ 3 raw alternatives named **including "do nothing"**, each with a failure hypothesis (researched + validated at P1.S5).
- Money proximity stated — who pays today to avoid or patch this pain (named behavior, tool spend, or budget line); a pain with no current spend and no budget owner FAILS.
- Disqualifier written: what would have to be true for this to NOT work.

**Gate out (mandatory — see SKILL.md house rules):**
1. Draft `P1-Ideation.S2-Problem.md`.
2. Invoke `/critical` on the draft — attack the "why now" claim, the evidence, and the "why isn't this solved" reasoning hardest.
3. Revise; note what changed.
4. Present the draft + the critical findings + the changes to the owner. **Wait for their explicit approval. Never auto-advance.**

**Checkpoint (active):** "Here's the problem statement: [X]. Before ICP — is this the problem you're betting on, or is there a deeper problem underneath? Pick one to commit to."
